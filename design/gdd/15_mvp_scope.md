## 15. MVP Scope

The MVP is about 3 hours of play that takes one carrot through the whole loop: grown, merged, delivered, researched into an ancient species, and finally watered by a spirit the player woke.

### MVP simplifications

| Full design | MVP version | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bram and Wren own the spirit quests | Ilse gives one combined quest at level 8 | Three NPCs only |
| Three sites with layers | One 6×6 single-layer site with one husk, Root-sense included | Validates the dig mechanic cheaply |
| Revival Potion needs Moonbell and Honey | 1 Spirit Essence (found in the dig) + 2 Sun Bouquet + 100 coins | Neither ingredient exists in the MVP |
| Spirit Workshop and Alchemy Hut | One Spirit Bench does both | One building, one screen |
| Sap Press | Sap crafted by hand | Upkeep is tested without another building |
| Four lucky items | Clover only | One modifier proves the formula |

### Deliberately out of the MVP

Quality steps, fertilizer, crop and animal traits, breeding, mutation, weather, seasons, events, Mill, Kitchen, hives, pigs, special requests, Codex rewards, embryos, Farm Level, the Village Market and Ancient Forest maps, controller remapping, localisation.

### What the MVP must prove

- The chore loop is pleasant for 30 minutes with no automation.

- Players choose to merge at 65% without feeling cheated.

- Players understand research without a text tutorial.

- Waking the Rootling is the session's high point, and players immediately want a second spirit.
