## 16. Future Feature Roadmap

After the MVP, content arrives in four releases, each adding one phase of the progression and each playable on its own.

| Release | Adds | Depends on | Proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 "Living Farm" (Phase 2) | Quality steps, fertilizer, weather, seasons, Mill, Kitchen, hives, 5 more crops, special requests, all four lucky items, Village Market map, Farm Level | MVP | The farm stays interesting for 8 hours |
| R2 "Bloodlines" (Phase 3) | Traits, mutation, Breeding Pen, pigs, Odette, Genetic Laboratory, embryos, Sunhen and Cloudfleece, Epic recipes, full Codex with rewards, 4 blueprint crops | R1 quality and special requests | Collection is a long-term goal |
| R3 "The Old Forest" (Phase 4) | Ancient Forest map, Bram and Wren, three sites with layers, full restoration, Alchemy Hut, Spirit Workshop levels 1–3, Leafling, Bloomling, Sap Press, Glowcap | R2 research levels | Excavation and restoration hold up when repeated |
| R4 "Spirit Farm" (Phase 5), version 1.0 | Chickling, Moo Spirit, Merchant Spirit, Elder spirits, grafts, Workshop levels 4–6, Legendary and Mythical recipes, Starlit Gourd, Worldroot, four seasonal events | R3 | Automation changes how the farm is played without ending it |
| After 1.0 | Tinkerling, more magical animals, more NPC storylines, more production chains, a fourth site, mobile port with capped offline progress, localisation | 1.0 sales | – |

A release starts only when the one before it passes its "proves" test with at least 10 outside players.
