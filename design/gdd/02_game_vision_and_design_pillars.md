## 2. Game Vision and Design Pillars

**Vision:** a farm that grows from two hands to a living ecosystem, where every rare thing the player finds opens a door instead of filling a shelf.

| # | Pillar | What it means in practice | A feature fails the pillar if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Everything feeds something | Every output is an input to at least two other systems | Its items are only ever sold |
| 2 | Rare means different, not slower | Rarity adds traits, quality and gene yield; growth time is set per species | Its only cost is waiting |
| 3 | Luck you can lean on | Every random roll shows its odds, has a pity rule and can be improved by the player | The player cannot change the odds |
| 4 | No lost progress | Animals do not die, crops wither only after a full dry day, orders carry no penalty | A mistake costs more than the last 10 minutes |
| 5 | Automation is a reward | Spirits remove chores the player has already mastered; decisions stay with the player | The game is tedious without spirits, or plays itself with them |
| 6 | Small world, deep systems | Four maps, built to be dense rather than large | It needs a new map |

**Tone:** warm, rustic, slightly mysterious. The forest is old and kind, not dangerous. There is no combat.
