## 1. Executive Summary

Mosswake Farm is a cozy pixel-art farming game in which the player grows a hand-worked plot into a magical farm run with the help of restored Tree Spirits (Ma Cây).

- **Genre:** farming simulation with recipe merging, gene research and worker automation.

- **Audience:** players of cozy farming and management games, 16+, sessions of 20–60 minutes.

- **Hook:** rare produce is not just sold. It is researched into genes, genes unlock ancient species, and the forest's buried spirits take over the chores the player has outgrown.

- **Signature feature:** Tree Spirits are dug up, cleaned, revived and given a vocation by the player, then assigned to zones drawn on the farm.

- **Scope at 1.0:** 20 crops, 6 animal species, 6 NPCs, 7 spirit classes, 3 excavation sites, about 25 hours to reach Phase 5.

- **MVP:** 5 crops, 3 animals, 18 goods, 10 merge recipes, 3 NPCs, 1 research chain, 1 spirit class. About 3 hours of play. Built in 7 milestones over about 22 weeks.

- **Main risk:** five interlocking systems. The roadmap controls it by shipping one vertical slice per system, each of which must be fun before the next starts.
